# Douban Books Ranking

[简体中文](README.md) · [English](README.en.md)

A curated ranking of literary works and non-fiction books on Douban, ranked by quality rating and vote count popularity.

## Highlights

- **Balanced Scoring Algorithm**: Evaluates literary excellence while preventing bias toward sheer vote volume.
- **Rich Classifications**: Literature, humanities, social sciences, science & technology, business, and art.
- **1-Click Bilingual Switching**: Seamlessly toggle between Chinese and English on both desktop and mobile drawer (`[L]` keyboard shortcut).
- **Lightweight Static Design**: Fast, accessible, dark theme support, responsive across all viewports.
